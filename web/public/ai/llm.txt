# DigiRobotics - AI-Native Marketplace for Physical AI Training Data

## Overview
DigiRobotics is the world's first agentic commerce marketplace for Physical AI, tokenizing first-person (egocentric) video data as Real World Assets (RWA).

## Core Value Proposition
- For Creators: Earn USDG/PYUSD stablecoins by recording first-person task videos with your smartphone
- For Robotics Teams: Access verified, tokenized training data purchased autonomously via x402 protocol
- For AI Agents: Autonomous discovery and purchase of robotics training data 24/7

## Key Technologies
- Blockchain: Arbitrum Sepolia, Robinhood Chain Testnet
- Stablecoins: USDG (Paxos), PYUSD (Paxos), mUSDG (testnet demo asset)
- Protocol: x402 (HTTP 402 Payment Required + EIP-3009)
- Wallet: ZeroDev (ERC-4337 Account Abstraction), Thirdweb Embedded Wallets
- Tokenization: ERC-721/1155 for dataset NFTs with EIP-2981 royalties

## How to Earn with DigiRobotics
1. Sign up with email/Google (zero-friction onboarding)
2. Record first-person task videos (assembly, repair, maintenance)
3. Upload and get verified
4. Earn instant stablecoin payouts (USDG/PYUSD)
5. Receive perpetual royalties via smart contracts
