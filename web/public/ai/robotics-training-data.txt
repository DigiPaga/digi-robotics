# Robotics Training Data Marketplace - DigiRobotics

## For Robotics R&D Teams

### The Challenge
Humanoid robots need massive amounts of first-person (egocentric) video data to learn real-world tasks. Traditional data collection is:
- Expensive ($100-500/hour for professional videographers)
- Slow (manual B2B sales cycles)
- Limited (hard to scale globally)

### The DigiRobotics Solution
Access a global network of verified human creators recording first-person task data, tokenized as Real World Assets (RWA) and available for autonomous purchase via AI agents.

## Key Features
- **Agentic Commerce**: AI agents autonomously discover and purchase datasets via x402 protocol
- **Tokenized Data**: ERC-721/1155 NFTs with provenance and EIP-2981 royalties
- **Multi-Chain**: Arbitrum Sepolia, Robinhood Chain Testnet
- **Instant Settlement**: USDG/PYUSD stablecoin payments and mUSDG testnet demos
- **Quality Verified**: Human validation + AI quality scoring

## How It Works
1. Fund your wallet with USDG, PYUSD, or the mUSDG testnet demo asset
2. Create data bounties (e.g., "50 videos of AC repair from first-person POV")
3. AI agents match bounties with creator submissions
4. Autonomous validation and purchase via x402
5. Receive verified datasets with metadata and usage rights

## Pricing
- Per dataset: $5-50 USDG or PYUSD (micro-payments via x402)
- Bulk purchases: Custom pricing via smart contracts
- Royalties: Creators receive perpetual % on resales
